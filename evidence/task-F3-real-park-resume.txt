ROOT CAUSE (read from source, confirmed by the above real run):
  cmd/made/daemon.go gateNotifyPushHandler builds the WorkFunc with:
    orchestrator.NewWorkFunc(rm, reviewDecisions, emit, runID, defaultBranch, branch, orchestrator.Options{})
  orchestrator.Options{} is the zero value in production - Options.DocumentRules is always nil.
  internal/orchestrator/workfunc.go documentStage() calls:
    document.Run(c.rc.Worktree.Path, c.defaultBranch, c.opts.DocumentRules)
  ...reading c.opts.DocumentRules (always nil in production), NOT c.rc.Config.Document.Rules
  (which IS correctly loaded from .made.yml by internal/config.LoadEffectiveConfig and IS present
  on RunContext.Config - it is simply never read by the orchestrator or converted from
  config.DocumentRule{PathPattern,RequiredDocPattern} to document.Rule{SourcePattern,DocPattern}).
  grep confirms zero production references:

This means: a real operator configuring document.rules in .made.yml today gets silent,
total non-enforcement of that policy in production - the Document stage always runs with
zero rules no matter what is configured. This is a genuine wiring gap, not a test artifact.
